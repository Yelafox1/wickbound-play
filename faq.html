Wickbound FAQ

Your light is your life. One run takes about 10 minutes.

PlayGive feedback

Controls

  • WASD or arrows: move. Weapons fire on their own.
  • Tap Space: Flare. Costs 20 wax, every weapon fires at once and the horde is thrown back.
  • Hold Space: Blaze. Double fire rate and a wider light while it burns wax.
  • Esc: pause. 1–3: pick a level-up card. On touch, drag to move and use the flame button.

The wax rule

  • Wax is your health, your light and your power, all in one bar.
  • Your light shrinks as wax burns down. Weapons only hit what is inside your light.
  • Kills in your light, Kiln Wights (elites), candle shrines and killing the boss relight you.
  • At 0 wax you are Guttering: time slows for 4 seconds. Kill 8 in the ember to relight. Once per run.

Bright vs Ember

  • Bright (gold cards): hits harder the fuller your wax. At 70%+ wax you are Holy: kills relight 50% more, Flare becomes a bigger holy blast, and you deal +15% damage to the Pale Bell.
  • Ember (red cards): hits harder the lower your wax. At 30% or less you are Kindled: Ember hits set foes alight and Blaze costs 60% less. With an Ember card you also take 50% less damage and Ember kills relight 2x. Kindled fire burns the Pale Bell too, a little weaker than on other foes.
  • The meter next to the wax bar shows whichever bonus is stronger right now.
  • Against the Pale Bell, stacked damage bonuses (lean, Devoted, Holy) are capped at x2.55 total.
  • Devoted: hold 3 cards of one lean and none of the other (the starting Censer doesn't count) for +35% damage vs the boss and elites, +15% for each extra card of that lean, up to +75%. Neutral cards never count or break it. Mixing leans removes it. Level-up cards are tagged when they complete or break it.
  • If your lean's cards are all maxed or your slots are full, level-ups fill with neutral cards (Candle Stub, Tallow Draught, Alms Box) so you always have at least two picks that keep Devoted.

Weapons

  • Bright: Censer Swing (starter arc), Sun Ring (orbiting flames), Vigil Lance (piercing spear, seeks the Bell), Vesper Beam (steady beam on the strongest foe; stronger the fuller your wax).
  • Ember: Cinder Brand (burning pools), Last Rites (smites the toughest foe), Snuffer's Chain (leaping embers), Ash Wake (point-blank burst, wider at low wax; burning foes it hits erupt).

What is in this slice

One yard (the Parish), one keeper (the Sexton), 8 weapons, 2 evolutions and the Pale Bell boss at 8:00, in a 10-minute run. Silence the Bell and survive to dawn to win.
